01 The idea

Two ways your mind makes decisions

Who

Daniel Kahneman

A psychologist who won the 2002 Nobel Prize in Economics for studying how people really decide.

Where

Thinking, Fast and Slow

His 2011 book. It took the idea far beyond psychology.

The words

Stanovich and West

The labels System 1 and System 2 came from psychologists Keith Stanovich and Richard West. Kahneman made them famous.

System 1 fast and automatic System 2 slow and deliberate

A way to think about thinking, not two parts of the brain.

01 Fast and slow

Some decisions can't wait for a plan.

You use both kinds of thinking every time you drive.

System 1
The reflex
Instant. Automatic. No time to think.
Ball rolls into the roadBrake
Car ahead stopsBrake
Light turns amberEase off
Reacts in a blink
System 2
The route planner
Slow. Deliberate. Deep.
Unfamiliar city, road closed ahead
Read the map
Compare the routes
Weigh traffic and tolls
Pick the route
Worth the time, when there is time
Don't ask the planner to brake. Software has the same problem. Some decisions must be instant, and a reasoning model is too slow to make them.
