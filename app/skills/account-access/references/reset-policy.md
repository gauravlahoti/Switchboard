# Password reset and lockout policy

- An account locks after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
- A reset unlocks the account and sends a one-time link to the registered work email. The link is valid for 30 minutes.
- A reset requires an enrolled authenticator (MFA). Without MFA the reset goes through Identity and Access.
- A reset only ever applies to the person asking. Nobody resets a password for someone else through this desk.
- The helpdesk phone line is x5500 for anyone who cannot receive the link.
